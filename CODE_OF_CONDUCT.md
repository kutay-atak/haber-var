# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and patient with people who are new to the project or to programming.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people who use it.
- Respecting the privacy of the groups that run Haber Var: never share data from a real newspaper in an issue or pull request.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Insults, personal or political attacks, and sexualised language or imagery.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information (addresses, emails, screenshots of private newspapers) without permission.
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the maintainer, Kutay Atak, through a direct message on GitHub or by opening an issue marked *private matter* that contains only a request to be contacted (not the details). All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they deem inappropriate.
